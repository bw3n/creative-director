## Agent's first read

The output of the brief-critique job:

```
RESTATE: A Singapore Flyer activation/campaign tied to Singapore's 60th anniversary of independence. Open brief means creative latitude — the client has not specified format, audience, or success criteria.

HUMAN TRUTH: Singapore at 60 is a moment of self-recognition, not celebration. The country is mature enough to look back without nostalgia and forward without anxiety.

TENSION: Individual Singaporean vs. collective Singapore. The brief's audience is millions of people with different races, languages, religions, and ages — but the campaign has to feel like *one* Singapore without flattening any of those differences.

CLICHÉ: The national-day platitude. "We are Singapore." Children in flag colours. Parade shots. The Marina Bay skyline at golden hour. The brief is gesturing at this default; the work must escape it.

GAPS:
- No format. Print, OOH, social, activation, broadcast, or hybrid? Each shapes the work differently.
- No audience priority. Residents, tourists, or diaspora first?
- No success criteria. Awareness, sentiment, footfall, ticket sales?
- No brand codes named. Singapore Flyer's own visual/verbal codes are not in the brief.

CHEAPEST READ: Singapore at 60 is not a moment of arrival; it is a moment of arrival-in-progress. The Flyer's wheel — already turning, already up there — is the perfect emblem of a country that is *still going up*.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP: Ideation (3 territories), then brainstorm expansion on the strongest. Skip art direction until a territory is locked.
```

## Lessons

What this brief teaches the corpus:

- **The category cliché is real.** "We are Singapore" + children in flag colours is the national-day default. The brief card captures this so future briefs in this category inherit the warning.
- **Brand codes matter.** Singapore Flyer's wheel is a ready-made emblem. Without a brand card, the agent wouldn't know that.
- **Tension-first briefs.** The agent's first move (naming the tension) is the same move it makes for any brief. This brief card captures the move so future briefs follow the pattern.

If this brief were real, the next step would be: write the category card for `national-day-campaigns` from this brief, write the brand card for `singapore-flyer` if it doesn't exist, then run ideation.
