===============================================================================

November 26, 2025

![SENNHEISER MOMENTUM 4 Wireless headphone, Sennheiser Sleepers](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sennheiser-momentum-4-wireless-headphone.webp "Sennheiser Sleepers: The MOMENTUM 4 Headphone Campaign That Turns Naps Into Art 1")

Sennheiser is taking the daily grind of public transport and turning it into a delightfully relatable creative moment. In its latest campaign, “Battery That Lasts Longer Than Yours,” developed by Ogilvy, the brand taps into a universal commuter truth: at some point, we all surrender to an unplanned nap.

![Sennheiser Sleepers](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sennheiser-momentum-4-wireless-headphone_3.jpg "Sennheiser Sleepers: The MOMENTUM 4 Headphone Campaign That Turns Naps Into Art 2")

The campaign’s visuals, captured in moody black-and-white photography, show commuters peacefully knocked out on trains and metros—bags slipping, ties loosened, reality paused. Yet one thing remains fully alert: the Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless headphones. With an impressive 60-hour battery life, these headphones keep streaming long after the human wearing them has checked out.
