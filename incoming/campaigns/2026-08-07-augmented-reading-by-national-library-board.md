### Experience the future of storytelling with Augmented Reading by the National Library Board, powered by Snap Spectacles—where books come alive through immersive sights and sounds.

Imagine reading your favorite novel and suddenly hearing the footsteps of a mysterious character or seeing magical visuals unfold right before your eyes. Thanks to an exciting collaboration between the National Library Board (NLB) Singapore, Snap Inc., and creative agency LePub Singapore, that dream is becoming reality. This innovative project introduces Augmented Reading, an immersive experience powered by Snap Spectacles and Snap OS, which transforms how people engage with books using augmented reality (AR).

Currently in beta testing, this groundbreaking initiative is expected to roll out later this year, offering a glimpse into the future of storytelling where technology meets imagination.

![Augmented Reading by Snap Spectacles, Campaigns of the world](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Augmented-Reading-by-Snap-Spectacles.webp "Step Into Stories with Augmented Reading by Snap Spectacles & National Library Board Singapore 2")

#### The Vision Behind Augmented Reading

At its core, the Augmented Reading experience aims to reinvent the reading journey by combining traditional text with real-time audiovisual enhancements. Readers wearing Snap Spectacles will not only read the words on a page but also experience accompanying soundscapes, ambient music, visual effects, and interactive elements that bring stories to life.

This initiative is part of the National Library Board’s broader mission to inspire learning
