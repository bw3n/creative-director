![Transparency Card, Corruption in Brazilian politics, Campaigns of the world](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Transparency_Card_thumb.webp "Eyes on the Expenses: Transparency Card Revolutionizes Political Accountability in Brazil 1")

### Transparency Card Campaign Shines a Light on Political Spending in Brazil.

When it comes to holding public officials accountable, access to transparent information is non-negotiable. In Brazil, politicians receive public funds to cover work-related expenses such as travel, food, and car rentals. However, the misuse of these funds has contributed to an estimated annual loss of $40 billion due to corrupt spending. While Brazilians have the constitutional right to monitor how these funds are used, the complexity of government data makes it nearly impossible for the average citizen to track effectively.

This lack of accessibility is where innovation steps in. With a clever and citizen-focused approach, [AKQA](https://www.akqa.com/work/congresso-em-foco/transparency-card/) partnered with Congresso em Foco, an independent digital news portal in Brazil, to launch the Transparency Card. This groundbreaking initiative leverages real-time technology to shed light on political spending and empower citizens to take an active role in monitoring public funds.

#### Simplifying Transparency with the Transparency Card:

Imagine being able to track a politician’s expenses as easily as you track your own credit card transactions. The Transparency Card makes this possible. Designed to mimic the familiar format of personal financial notifications, the card taps into government databases and provides real-time updates on how politicians spend taxpayers’ money.
