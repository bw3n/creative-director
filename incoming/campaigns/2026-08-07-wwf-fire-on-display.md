Fire on Display: WWF Turns TV Showrooms Into a Wake-Up Call for Wildfires
=========================================================================

July 25, 2026

![WWF Fire on Display Campaign](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wwf-fire-on-display.webp "Fire on Display: WWF Turns TV Showrooms Into a Wake-Up Call for Wildfires 1")

When shoppers walk into an electronics store, television displays are expected to showcase vibrant landscapes, crystal-clear oceans and colourful nature scenes. WWF challenged that expectation with Fire on Display, an [OOH campaign](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/ooh-campaigns/) created by Nossa that transformed ordinary TV showrooms into a powerful reminder of Portugal’s recurring wildfire crisis.

Launched in Portugal during July 2026, the campaign partnered with Fnac and Darty to replace traditional demonstration footage on televisions across 52 stores with real images of some of the country’s largest wildfires from recent years. Instead of selling the beauty of nature, the screens revealed what can happen when that landscape is lost.

The creative idea is remarkably simple because it uses the medium itself as the message. Retail televisions are designed to impress customers with vivid colours, sharp contrast and exceptional picture quality. Fire on Display uses those same technical strengths to present devastating wildfire footage, creating an unexpected contrast that immediately changes the shopping experience.

Unlike many awareness campaigns that ask audiences to visit a dedicated exhibition or event, Fire on Display reaches people during an everyday retail visit. Customers comparing television models suddenly find themselves watching scenes that feel completely out of place in a showroom. That unexpected context gives the campaign its emotional impact without relying on complex technology or large physical installations.
