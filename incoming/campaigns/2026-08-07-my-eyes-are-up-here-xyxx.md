My Eyes Are Up Here: How XYXX Turned a Simple Observation Into a Conversation
=============================================================================

June 16, 2026

![XYXX My Eyes Are Up Here campaign featuring the role-reversal concept](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/my-eyes-are-up-here-xyxx-campaign.webp "My Eyes Are Up Here: How XYXX Turned a Simple Observation Into a Conversation 1")

My Eyes Are Up Here takes a simple observation and turns it into a conversation starter. Created by men’s lifestyle brand [XYXX](https://xyxxcrew.com/) in collaboration with content creator Vir Saini, the campaign approaches an everyday social behaviour from an unexpected angle, using humour to encourage audiences to reconsider something many people rarely question.

According to the brand, the film crossed 5 million organic views and generated more than 100,000 engagements within 48 hours of launch, sparking discussions and audience interaction across social media.  
![XYXX My Eyes Are Up Here campaign featuring the role-reversal concept](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/my-eyes-are-up-here-xyxx-campaign.webp "My Eyes Are Up Here: How XYXX Turned a Simple Observation Into a Conversation 2")

The Insight Behind the Campaign
-------------------------------

The starting point for the campaign came from an observation linked to XYXX’s Invisible Vest. As the brand explained, many men had never really considered visible vest lines in their daily lives.

That seemingly small detail opened the door to a broader cultural insight. Rather than focusing on the product alone, the campaign explores how appearance-related judgments often become normalised over time, to the point where they are no longer questioned.

Instead of presenting that idea directly, the campaign chooses a more engaging route.
