DTDC Ke SuperDads Turns a Delivery Executive Into the Superhero of a Child’s Story
==================================================================================

June 23, 2026

![DTDC Ke SuperDads Father's Day campaign featuring a child describing his delivery executive father as a superhero](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/dtdc-ke-superdads-delivery-hero.webp "DTDC Ke SuperDads Turns a Delivery Executive Into the Superhero of a Child’s Story 1")

DTDC Ke SuperDads takes a familiar Father’s Day theme and gives it a perspective that feels both personal and refreshingly grounded. Rather than focusing on grand gestures or emotional declarations, the film looks at India’s delivery professionals through the eyes of the people who know them best: their families.

Every day, delivery executives enter millions of homes, often remembered only for the package they bring. Their interactions are brief, functional, and easy to overlook. DTDC’s latest Father’s Day film shifts attention away from the delivery itself and toward the person behind it.  
 

DTDC Ke SuperDads Finds Heroism in Everyday Work
------------------------------------------------

At the heart of the film is a simple insight. While customers know delivery professionals for the job they do, their families see something entirely different.

The story follows a young boy participating in a school speech competition where he talks about his favourite superhero. His description sounds like that of a larger-than-life character. Someone who crosses mountains, travels at incredible speed, and carries a magical bag capable of bringing happiness wherever he goes.

As the narrative unfolds, the audience discovers that the superhero is not a fictional character at all. It is his father, a delivery executive.

The reveal works because it reframes ordinary responsibilities through a child’s imagination. The daily realities of the job become heroic adventures, creating a perspective that many viewers may never have considered before.
