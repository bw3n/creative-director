### AI calls for more women – “AFTERWORK” movie by [Artefact 3000](https://artefact3000.com/) and WomenAtArtefact

The underrepresentation of women in [artificial intelligence](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/tag/artificial-intelligence/) (AI) remains a pressing issue, with only 19% of senior management positions in AI held by women. Addressing this disparity, Women@Artefact, an initiative dedicated to advancing women’s careers in tech, has joined forces with Artefact 3000, the group’s creative agency, to launch a compelling film titled “Afterwork.” This unique creation merges social commitment with technological innovation to amplify the voices of women in artificial intelligence.

#### A Powerful Narrative Through AI Technology

“Afterwork” is an original short film crafted entirely using AI technologies. It features three women engaged in a lively discussion over drinks about their careers in the tech industry. However, these women are not just any professionals—they represent iconic virtual assistants who voice their frustrations about the roles they are confined to in AI.

One poignant line from the film captures this sentiment: “If you had told me I would become a tech star by being an assistant…” This reflection sheds light on the significant gender imbalance in AI leadership and the need for greater female representation in the field.

The film concludes with a powerful call to action: “AI calls for more women. Let’s give them a bigger voice.” This message underscores the urgency of promoting [gender diversity in AI](https://www.unwomen.org/en/articles/explainer/artificial-intelligence-and-gender-equality) and fostering an inclusive environment where women can thrive.
