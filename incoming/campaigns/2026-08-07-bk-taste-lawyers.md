#### BK Taste Lawyers: A New Flame-Grilled Contender Arrives

The star of the campaign is the BK® Taste, a [flame-grilled burger](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/creative-print-ads/flame-grilled-burger-king-ads/) available in 1, 2, or 3 patty versions and served with a new smoky Taste Sauce. It launched nationwide across Brazil, offering something familiar yet distinctly “BK”—affordable, customizable, and packed with grilled flavor.  
But the way Burger King chose to introduce this item is what earned the spotlight.

#### A Campaign Rooted in a Rivalry

The fast-food world has long been known for its iconic rivalries, and this campaign leans right into that history.  
Over the years, many consumers have pointed out similarities between BK’s new release and a competitor’s well-known burger. But what most people don’t know is that the competitor’s burger was originally created to mimic the Whopper. Back in the early 2000s, the Whopper’s popularity pushed other chains to engineer their own “big, smoky, grilled-style” sandwiches to keep up.
