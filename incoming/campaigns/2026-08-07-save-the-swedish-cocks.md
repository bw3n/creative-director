### Save The Swedish Cocks – A Satirical Campaign with a Serious Message

#### A Bold Wake-Up Call from the World of Creative Activism

Sweden has long enjoyed a reputation as a global leader in progressive values—championing sustainability, human rights, and animal welfare. But a disturbing truth lurks behind its image: every year, over 7 million male chicks are killed in the Swedish egg industry simply because they’re male and don’t lay eggs. These animals are not used for meat, nor is their culling tied to health regulations—they’re killed purely due to economic inefficiency.

![Save The Swedish Cocks, Campaigns of the world](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Save-The-Swedish-Cocks.webp "Save The Swedish Cocks: The Provocative Campaign Shining a Light on Sweden’s Silent Slaughter 2")

Enter “Save The Swedish Cocks,” a provocative, darkly satirical campaign that doesn’t pull punches.

#### The Shocking Reality Behind Sweden’s Egg Industry

While countries like France, Germany, and the Netherlands have banned the practice of killing male chicks, Sweden—ironically seen as a role model in animal welfare—still allows it. The reason? Male chicks in the egg industry are considered waste. They don’t produce eggs and are not economically viable for meat production. So, they’re culled en masse—often on the day they hatch.

This little-known truth is what the campaign aims to bring into the public eye.
