![Makro Adscan, Campaigns of the world](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/makro-adscan.webp "Makro’s ADSCAN: The Clever Retail Hack That Turns Every Ad Into a Discount 1")

### Makro ADSCAN turns every brand ad into a discount, using creativity—not budget—to reinvent how shoppers buy.

Picture the supermarket industry as a game where the big players scatter stores across every corner like confetti. Now imagine Makro—leaner, smaller, and armed with only 22 stores—trying to keep up while competitors enjoy gigantic ad budgets. With just 4% of the category’s media spend, Makro was playing on hard mode.

But instead of trying to shout louder, Makro decided to think sharper.

And that’s how ADSCAN was born — not as a typical campaign, but as a disruptive retail tool that hijacks the entire advertising landscape and flips it in Makro’s favor.

#### The Underdog Problem: Competing Against Giants With Deep Pockets

Let’s imagine the supermarket game as a race. Most big chains sprint ahead with endless stores and heavy ad investments. Makro, on the other hand, starts far behind—fewer stores, smaller footprint, and a budget that looks tiny compared to the rest of the category.

**The numbers say it all:**

* The category has 3,688 total stores. Makro has 22.
* Competitors spend USD 34.5MM in media. Makro spends USD 1.4MM.

So how does a challenger brand stay relevant?
