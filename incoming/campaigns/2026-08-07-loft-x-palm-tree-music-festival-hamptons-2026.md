LOFT x Palm Tree Music Festival Hamptons 2026
=============================================

July 10, 2026

![LOFT x Palm Tree Music Festival Hamptons 2025](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/loft-x-palm-tree-music-festival-hamptons-2025-COTW.webp "LOFT x Palm Tree Music Festival Hamptons 2026 1")

Following the success of its inaugural Summer of LOFT campaign, LOFT expanded its festival presence to the East Coast with its first-ever activation at [Palm Tree Music Festival Hamptons](https://palmtreemusicfestival.com/hamptons/). The activation was designed to connect with music and fashion enthusiasts, introduce the brand to new audiences, and further establish LOFT within the cultural conversation.
