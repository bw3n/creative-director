Burger King’s ‘Leave The Flame Grilling To Us’ Campaign Gives Summer Sunburn a Humorous Twist
=============================================================================================

August 4, 2026

![Burger King Leave The Flame Grilling To Us Campaign](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/burger-king-leave-the-flame-grilling-to-us.webp "Burger King’s ‘Leave The Flame Grilling To Us’ Campaign Gives Summer Sunburn a Humorous Twist 1")

Burger King Leave The Flame Grilling To Us taps into the growing “tanmaxxing” trend with a witty summer campaign that turns painful sunburns into visual storytelling. Created by Happiness Brussels, the work draws a humorous parallel between overexposed skin and Burger King’s iconic flame-grilled cooking, delivering a simple reminder that the grilling is best left to the experts.

Instead of commenting on the trend directly, the campaign borrows familiar beachside moments that almost everyone recognises. Distinct tan lines become the creative device, linking seasonal experiences with the brand’s long-standing flame-grilled identity in a way that feels timely and easy to understand.

The visuals feature sharply defined sunburn patterns left by caps, sunglasses, necklaces and shorts, each transformed into a playful metaphor for being “flame grilled.” Paired with the line “Leave The Flame Grilling To Us,” the campaign lets the imagery do most of the storytelling, keeping the message immediate and memorable.
