World Unseen: The Photography Exhibition You Can Feel – Breaking Visual Barriers with Canon Technology

September 7, 2024

![Canon World Unseen, Campaigns of the world](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/canon-World-Unseen_cotw.webp "World Unseen: The Photography Exhibition You Can Feel – Breaking Visual Barriers with Canon Technology 1")

### Canon, a global leader in imaging technology, believes that photography is an art form that should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their ability to see. But how do you make something so inherently visual accessible to those with sight loss? Enter World Unseen, a groundbreaking photography exhibition that doesn’t just challenge the boundaries of traditional photography but completely redefines them.

Welcome to the photography exhibition you don’t need to see – designed for blind, partially sighted, and sighted people alike.

#### The Challenge: Making Photography Accessible to All

In the UK alone, there are over 2 million people living with sight loss. For these individuals, engaging with visual art, especially photography, is often an inaccessible experience. Canon recognized that while we live in a world saturated with images, those with visual impairments are left out of this form of artistic expression. The challenge was clear: how could Canon ensure that blind and partially sighted people could truly experience photography, not just be told about it?
