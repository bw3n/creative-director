Epica Awards Unveils Groundbreaking AIJE Advisor for 2024: A New Era in Creative Judging
========================================================================================

September 17, 2024

![Epica Awards, AI Jury, Campaigns of the world, AIJE Advisor](https://campaignsoftheworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/epica-awards-aije-advisor.webp "Epica Awards Unveils Groundbreaking AIJE Advisor for 2024: A New Era in Creative Judging 1")

The Epica Awards, renowned for being the only global creative competition judged by journalists, is set to unveil an exciting new tool for its 2024 jury. Following a successful trial last year, the awards will now incorporate an advanced AI-powered assistant to support the judging process. This innovation comes on the heels of the AI Jury Experiment (AIJE), which explored how AI could evaluate creativity.

#### The Evolution of AI in Creative Judging

In 2023, the Epica Awards introduced the AI Jury Experiment, where an AI system evaluated shortlisted entries based on text descriptions. This trial, while separate from the main competition, sparked a significant dialogue about AI’s potential in assessing creative work. The experiment allowed the Epica Awards and its press jury to engage with the concept of AI in the creative evaluation process.

#### Introducing AIJE Advisor: A New Era of Judging

Fast forward to 2024, and the AIJE has evolved considerably. The new tool, now rebranded as AIJE Advisor, is designed to analyze entries across all categories, including video and case studies. Nicolas Huvé, the Epica Awards Operations Director and the mastermind behind the AIJE, shared his thoughts on this development: “AIJE became so proficient that it was akin to an ‘Oppenheimer moment.’ Out of respect for our entrants and jurors, we decided it would be more responsible to use it as a support tool rather than as a primary judge.”
